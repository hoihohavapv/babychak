📲 How to Install the App

1
Download the App
Tap the download button below
2
Open Downloads Folder
Go to your phone's Files or Downloads app
3
Tap the APK File
Find loader.apk.apk and tap it
4
Allow Installation
If you see "Install blocked", tap Settings and enable "Allow from this source"
5
Tap "Install"
Wait for the app to install
6
Tap "Open"
After installation, tap "Open" to launch the app
